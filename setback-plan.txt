AUTOFLY SETBACK DETECTION PLAN
================================

PHASE 1 - RAMP UP (normal operation)
- Speed increases slowly each tick (rampUpIncrement)
- Setbacks start being detected and counted
- Display: "Setback 1/10", "Setback 2/10", etc.
- All timers are REAL LIFE milliseconds, not ticks

PHASE 2 - THRESHOLD REACHED (10 setbacks first time, 5 after that)
- Print "Setback detected" (no more "X/10" counting)
- Decrease speed by rampUpIncrement
- Enter COOLDOWN WATCH (5 real life seconds of no setbacks)

PHASE 3a - COOLDOWN SUCCEEDS (no setbacks for 5 real life seconds)
- Speed is safe for now, hold current speed
- Enter QUIET WATCH (3 real life minutes of no setbacks)

PHASE 4 - QUIET PERIOD SUCCEEDS (zero setbacks for 3 real life minutes)
- Server is stable, start slowly ramping back up
- Increase speed by rampUpIncrement / 3 per tick (1/3 normal ramp speed)
- Keep watching for setbacks with 5 setback threshold
- If setbacks detected during slow ramp, go back to PHASE 2

PHASE 3b - COOLDOWN FAILS (setback happens within 5 real life seconds)
- Server still pushing back, speed still too high
- Decrease speed again immediately
- Reset the 5 second cooldown timer
- Keep doing this until we get 5 quiet seconds
- After 5 quiet seconds, enter 3 minute quiet watch (PHASE 3a)

PHASE 5 - SUBSEQUENT CYCLES
- After the first full 10-setback cycle completes,
  all future cycles use 5 setbacks as the threshold
- Same pattern: 5 setbacks -> decrease -> 5s cooldown -> 3min quiet -> slow ramp

SUMMARY
- First cycle: 10 setbacks to trigger
- Every cycle after: 5 setbacks to trigger
- After every decrease: wait 5 real life seconds to hold speed
- If setback during cooldown: decrease immediately, reset timer
- After 5 quiet seconds: hold speed, start 3 minute quiet watch
- After 3 quiet minutes: ramp up at 1/3 normal speed
- If setback during slow ramp: back to decreasing with 5 threshold
- All timings are real life milliseconds
