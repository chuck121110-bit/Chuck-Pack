AUTOFLY SETBACK DETECTION PLAN
================================

PHASE 1 - RAMP UP (normal operation)
- Speed increases slowly each tick (rampUpIncrement every 250ms)
- Every setback is counted instantly
- Display: "Setback 1/10", "Setback 2/10", etc.
- All timers are REAL LIFE milliseconds, not ticks

PHASE 1 - THRESHOLD REACHED (10 setbacks)
- Print "10 setbacks!"
- Decrease speed by rampUpIncrement
- Speed NEVER increases again
- Enter PHASE 2

PHASE 2 - HOLD & WATCH (repeating)
- Hold current speed (no increase, no decrease on quiet)
- Watch for setbacks
- For EVERY setback: decrease speed by rampUpIncrement immediately
- Reset the 5 second quiet timer on each setback
- After 5 seconds of zero setbacks: just hold (don't ramp)
- Stay in PHASE 2 forever (more setbacks = more decreases)

SUMMARY
- Phase 1: Ramp up, count setbacks. At 10 setbacks → Phase 2
- Phase 2: Hold speed. Every setback decreases. 5s quiet holds.
- Speed can only go DOWN or stay the same after first 10 setbacks
- All timings are real life milliseconds
